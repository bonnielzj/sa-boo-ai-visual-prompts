# Composition Study Card

Use one card per study. Keep the selected campaign territory, core copy, and dominant
mechanism constant across the study set.

```yaml
study_id: C01
status: craft_study
territory: "..."
dominant_visual_event: "The poster ..."

reference_evidence:
  - source_or_asset_id: "..."
    attraction: "..."
    effect: "..."
    mechanism: "..."
    do_not_copy: "..."
    original_translation: "..."

fixed:
  - campaign idea
  - headline and copy hierarchy
  - approved identity assets
  - dominant mechanism

variable_under_test:
  primary: scale | crop | axis | overlap | negative_space | type_field_contact | figure_ground
  question: "What decision does this study resolve?"

composition:
  first_read: "..."
  reading_path: "..."
  dominant_scale_and_crop: "..."
  type_behavior: "..."
  structural_field: "..."
  supporting_cues: "..."
  identity_behavior: "..."
  negative_space_pressure: "..."

color_and_material:
  field: "..."
  counterforce: "..."
  neutral: "..."
  signal_optional: "..."
  medium_truth: "..."

proofs:
  thumbnail: pass | fail | not_tested
  squint: pass | fail | not_tested
  real_copy: pass | fail | not_tested
  logo_integration: pass | fail | not_tested
  target_format: pass | fail | not_tested

strength: "..."
risk: "..."
selection_reason_or_rejection: "..."
next_change_if_selected: "..."
```

## Comparison rule

Compare studies on the same canvas size with the same real copy and brand authority.
Do not improve one study's typography, texture, or rendering more than the others
before the spatial decision is made.

The chosen study should win because its dominant event, reading rhythm, and campaign
fit are stronger—not because it has more effects or a more polished mockup.
