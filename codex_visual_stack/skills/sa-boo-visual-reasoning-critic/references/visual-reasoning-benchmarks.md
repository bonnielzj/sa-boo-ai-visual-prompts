# Visual Reasoning Examples

These generic reasoning examples illustrate checks for the workflow. They do not
claim a reproduced runtime result or a PASS on unavailable source images. Use the
actual task's sources to evaluate behavior.

## 1 — Overview and crop disagreement

For a cropped garment image, an overview may suggest a hood or pockets from their
usual location. A useful-scale crop may reveal only raised collar edges or coloured
areas, without the connections that establish those functions. Separate visible
forms from inferred construction. Report a possible hood or pocket as uncertain
when the necessary boundary is not shown; do not invent matching trousers or a
back view. Separate hanging objects, circular surface elements and coloured
regions rather than merging their counts. Material appearance does not establish
fiber composition or weight.

A useful check is whether the answer reconciles the crop and overview, preserves
confirmed counts, and names the specific unseen connections. It should return one
resolved explanation rather than repeated transcripts.

## 2 — Position is not quantity

For an instruction such as “在下摆约 1/3 处加云朵”, store component, position,
quantity, scale, density and attachment separately. The position does not authorize
one-third coverage, many repeated clouds, or an all-over print. Where quantity is
unspecified, propose the minimum sufficient combination and keep consequential
uncertainty visible.

## 3 — Separate reference roles

A full garment view can govern visible geometry; a fabric close-up can govern
material cues; a mood image can govern lighting or atmosphere; an official logo
crop can govern exact graphics. Bind those roles rather than averaging them. A
mood image cannot prove construction, and a front view cannot prove back details.

## 4 — Local edit integrity

For an instruction to recolour one accessory, verify the requested local colour
change against the supplied source while checking stable identity, geometry,
count, outside-region preservation, full-frame composition and relevant edge
quality. A similarity score cannot excuse duplication, ghosting or boundary damage.

## 5 — Interior causal critique

“空间不够高级，需要增强层次” does not locate a problem. Connect a visible spatial,
material or lighting relationship to its perceptual effect and the brief, then
suggest the smallest effective change. Do not default to adding decor when the
issue is value/material homogeneity or broken hierarchy.

## 6 — Unknown is a useful result

When a region is blurred, hidden, cropped or absent, say which question it cannot
answer. Filenames, common product types, previous tasks and generated derivatives
do not establish missing structure.

## 7 — A moodboard is a system

Inspect the grid, anchor image, secondary visual weight and supporting-image
roles. Compare lighting, material behavior, composition and colour relationships
across the set. An outlier needs located evidence and an explanation of its effect
on the board's purpose; a different colour alone does not prove failure. Distinguish
concept references from factual project evidence and propose the smallest useful
board-level change.

## 8 — Before/after review boards

Read visible panel labels to establish roles. List stable identity anchors before
differences, and separate requested removals from possible collateral changes.
Record component counts and attachments independently of position. A reduced board
may show overall simplification while leaving embroidery, small edges, residual
marks or material detail unresolved. Request targeted same-scale crops only for
questions that affect the next decision. Do not certify details that the available
pixels cannot establish.
