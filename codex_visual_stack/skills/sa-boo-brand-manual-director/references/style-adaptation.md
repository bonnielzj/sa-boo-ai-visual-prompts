# Style Adaptation

## Process Constant, Style Variable

The same audit, approval, QA, and release discipline applies to every brand.
Typography, composition, pacing, materiality, image behavior, and book format
must change with the brand.

Do not begin with a named style such as "Swiss", "fashion magazine", "cute",
"luxury", or "tech". Translate the brand into controllable variables.

## Style Vector

Score only when useful:

| Variable | 0 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Authority | informal | institutional |
| Experimentation | conventional | disruptive |
| Warmth | cool | human |
| Play | serious | playful |
| Density | sparse | information-rich |
| Image dominance | text-led | image-led |
| Tactility | flat/digital | material/physical |
| Motion | static | kinetic/sequential |

Add cultural, category, accessibility, and production constraints in words.
Numbers are a comparison aid, not an aesthetic recipe.

## Style Contract

For each territory, state:

- strategic role;
- desired impression;
- anti-impression;
- grid behavior;
- type hierarchy;
- color rhythm;
- image ratio and crop behavior;
- graphic or material mechanism;
- page pacing;
- bilingual behavior;
- production implications;
- what remains fixed across all territories.

## Artist Worlds and Bilingual Composition

For artist/creator manuals, connect the persistent identity and evolving creative
worlds through attribution and page sequence. Related album art, original drawing,
character stories, and products may need distinct chapter openings and a concise
bridge. Do not force stylistically different originals into one equal-weight spread
merely because they share a song source. Preserve each authorized work's treatment.

Set a spread's focal point, viewing scale, margins, columns, and baseline relations
before placing its type. Test complete strings: Chinese and English may have unequal
but complementary roles. English can carry a strong editorial title or a quieter
explanatory counterweight, while Chinese supplies the voice and precise context.
Do not reduce English to decorative uppercase labels or omit needed introductory
copy in the name of minimalism. Use controlled space, measure, weight, and optical
alignment; a fixed size ratio alone does not prove the pairing works.

Preserve supplied logo and logotype artwork exactly. Manual typography may support
it, but cannot substitute for or restyle the identity source. Distinguish the artist's
songs/lyrics from the authorship of supplied drawings and generated derivatives.

## Equal-Test Method

Use this method only when editorial direction is unresolved.

Use identical content and source assets for:

1. Cover.
2. Strategy or identity spread.
3. Dense technical rule spread.
4. Application or production spread.

Change only the editorial system. This prevents a territory from winning
because it received better photography, stronger copy, or fewer difficult
rules.

Adapt the test set to the medium. A compact digital guide may use two screen
archetypes; a long print book may need all four. A fixed-style repair or
localization requires no territory competition.

## Territory Generation

Generate territories from tensions inside the brand. Examples:

- precision vs. warmth;
- heritage vs. contemporary use;
- fashion authority vs. curiosity;
- technical proof vs. emotional access;
- play vs. maturity;
- local culture vs. international legibility.

Each territory needs a different mechanism, not merely a different color.

## Template Fit Test

Assess:

- page format and binding;
- master-page logic;
- grid flexibility;
- image-frame behavior;
- text density tolerance;
- bilingual support;
- font and style dependencies;
- color and material fit;
- production and accessibility;
- rights to adapt and redistribute.

Choose:

- `REUSE` when structure and style fit strongly;
- `HYBRID` when useful rhythm or masters can be retained;
- `REBUILD` when the template imposes the wrong brand.

The supplied template is an input, not an authority.

## Anti-Generic Checks

- Would the manual still feel like this brand with the logo temporarily hidden?
- Does the densest rule page retain the brand character?
- Can the style survive without hero photography?
- Does the system work in the brand's priority applications?
- Are expressive moments controlled rather than decorative?
- Is the manual usable by the people who must apply it?
