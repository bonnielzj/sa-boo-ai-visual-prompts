# Lettering mechanisms · 中文字形机制

This text guide summarizes transferable design operations. It does not contain a
licensed image library or establish the appearance of any particular reference.
Inspect supplied or authorized reference images for the active task before
choosing a mechanism.

| Intended relationship | Construction operation | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| A phrase reads as one graphic | Establish the whole silhouette, then share compatible contours, interlock concavities and coordinate negative space | Preserve essential components and reading order; tighter tracking alone is insufficient |
| Curved connections and long terminals | Extend from existing stroke tangents and coordinate curvature and weight transitions | Inspect joins, counters and space between characters at viewing scale |
| Heavy compact lettering | Carve necessary white channels from a planned black mass | Compensate complex characters so density does not erase recognition |
| Perspective and downward deformation | Separate whole-group spatial transformation from local contour stretching | Retain recognition anchors and inspect Chinese components after deformation |
| Erosion or dispersed edges | Protect a readable core; control transition and outer fragments by direction | Surface effects must not replace requested structural redesign |
| Static motion | Use a continuous displacement field, repeated contours, density change or stroke-derived extension | Keep recognizable anchors; arbitrary rotation does not establish motion |
| Halftone or texture | Make dot size and density follow contours and negative spaces | Textural detail does not demonstrate a custom typeface or production feasibility |
| A typographic poster | Use scale, line breaks, pause, colour and negative space to organize copy | A successful layout does not establish redesigned glyph anatomy |

Choose the mechanism that serves the actual wording, purpose and application.
Transfer a reference's relationship rather than copying protected expression,
watermarks, author names or unrelated text. A Latin example can inform a group
transformation; it cannot establish correct Chinese components. Record observed
properties separately from hypotheses about authorship, software or process.
